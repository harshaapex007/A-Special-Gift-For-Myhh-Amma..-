Then came our funny little discussion about what I should call you. You told me not to call you “Akka” and to call you “Mona” instead. 04 · FROM TALKS TO A BOND 💞

We started talking more and more, sharing little things, laughing, and making memories through our conversations. Slowly, you became someone very close to my heart. 06 · YOU MATTER A LOT 🌟

Mona Akka, you have become a very important person in my life. I may not say it every time, and I may not always find the perfect words, but I truly value our bond. Thank you for every reply, every laugh, every caring moment, and every time you made me feel included. 07 · ALWAYS YOUR THAMMUDU 🎀

Thank you for entering my life, Mona. Out of all the people I could have met at Vedantu, I am genuinely grateful that our paths crossed.
